## Description: <br>
Apple Design is a design-guidance knowledge base for applying Apple-style interaction design, fluid motion, typography, translucent materials, and accessibility principles to Web UI reviews and frontend work. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and designers use this skill to review and build Web interfaces with Apple-inspired interaction behavior, motion, typography, translucent material, and accessibility guidance. It is most relevant for gesture-driven UI, spring and inertia animation, visual polish, and frontend implementation reviews. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger wording may steer ordinary UI, typography, or accessibility tasks toward Apple-style or fluid-interface recommendations even when that style was not requested. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill when Apple-style interaction, fluid motion, typography, accessibility, or UI review guidance is relevant, and treat its recommendations as reviewable design guidance. <br>
Risk: Motion, translucency, and gesture recommendations can be unsuitable for users who need reduced motion, reduced transparency, or higher contrast. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's accessibility guidance for reduced motion, reduced transparency, and contrast preferences when applying its visual and interaction recommendations. <br>
